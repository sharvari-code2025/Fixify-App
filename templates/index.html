Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical repair take?

Screen and battery replacements are typically completed within 2 to 4 hours. Motherboard repairs or water damage treatments take between 24 and 48 hours. Your technician sets an expected completion time during diagnosis.

Can I reject an estimate if the repair cost is too high?

Yes, absolutely. Our zero guesswork guarantee ensures no repair begins without your explicit digital approval. If you reject the estimate, the device is returned without unexpected repair charges.

Is my private data safe during repair?

We strongly recommend backing up your data before handover. Our technicians follow strict privacy protocols and diagnostic testing without accessing personal files or messaging apps.

How does doorstep pickup and delivery work?

When booking, select "Pickup & Delivery" and enter your address. An authorized delivery agent collects your smartphone, hands over a digital acknowledgment, and returns it once repaired.
